### 2. Why Existing Enterprise Tools Fail the Supervisory Problem

| Security Tooling Layer | Primary Function | Why It Fails the National Supervisory Mission |
|---|---|---|
| **SIEM** (Splunk, Elastic, QRadar) | Detects malicious technical indicators inside a single enterprise perimeter. | Cannot compare operational performance across peer sectors; blind to what is *missing* (negative space) when logging agents are silenced; cannot audit SOC investigator integrity. |
| **SOAR** (Cortex XSOAR, Phantom) | Automates internal incident response playbooks. | Operates on individual alert triggers without cross-entity peer context; cannot detect systemic operational negligence or uninvestigated alert backlogs. |
| **UEBA** (Exabeam, Securonix) | Profiles internal employee behavioral anomalies. | Designed for insider threat profiling rather than national critical security entity supervisory posture assessment. |
| **SAT-SA (Supervisory Analytics)** | Evaluates the **integrity, completeness, and risk** of multi-CSE cybersecurity defense operations. | **Fills the missing supervisory layer:** Discovers execution gaps, exposes negative-space monitoring blind spots, and produces legally defensible, cryptographically signed audit snapshots. |
